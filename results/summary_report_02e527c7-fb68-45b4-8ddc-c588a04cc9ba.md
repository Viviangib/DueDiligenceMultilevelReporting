### Alignment Categories

- **Not Applicable**: The regulation does not address the topic of this indicator of the assessed framework OR this indicator is out of scope of the regulation.
- **Not Aligned/Not Covered**: This indicator is required by the regulation but is absent in the assessed framework, or it contradicts the regulation.
- **Partially Aligned**: The indicator includes some regulatory requirements but lacks critical elements essential to fulfilling the regulation's intent.
- **Mostly Aligned**: The indicator mostly matches the regulatory requirements with only minor differences or omissions.
- **Fully Aligned**: The indicator fully matches or is equivalent to regulatory requirements, covering the same scope and extent without deviation.

### Observations

- **Strengths**: The Cotton Standard generally shows strong alignment in its approach to pesticide management and environmental sustainability. Most indicators align with the EUDR in terms of commitments to reduce hazardous substances and promote sustainable practices.
  
- **Weaknesses**: Some indicators exhibit partial alignment due to a limited scope that does not fully capture comprehensive aspects such as climate adaptation/mitigation and broader socio-economic benefits (e.g., living wages).

- **Critical Gaps**: Identified gaps are mainly in areas demanding more comprehensive strategic approaches and detailing, including explicit procedural requirements and integration of broader environmental and socio-economic objectives as stipulated by the regulation.

## Glossary

- **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**: A strategy that uses a combination of techniques including biological control, habitat manipulation, and use of resistant varieties to manage pest populations at acceptable levels.
- **Highly Hazardous Pesticides (HHPs)**: Pesticides recognized to be particularly harmful to human health or the environment.
- **Living Wage**: A wage that is sufficient to maintain a safe, decent standard of living within the community.
