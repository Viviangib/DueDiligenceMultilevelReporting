## Recommendations

1. **Conduct Expert Review**: It is recommended that an expert panel review the areas identified as 'partially aligned' or 'not covered' to develop strategies for full compliance with EUDR requirements.

2. **Expand Scope of Practices**: Consider broadening the scope of current practices to include specific, demonstrable commitments toward climate change adaptation and mitigation, soil health improvement, and comprehensive stakeholder engagement.

3. **Enhance Training**: Implement more comprehensive training modules focusing on non-chemical methods of pest management and climate change adaptation techniques to improve awareness among producers and workers.

4. **Strengthen Monitoring Systems**: Enhance the current monitoring systems to ensure they are equipped to address the whole lifecycle of operations, including social, economic, and environmental impacts beyond pesticide management.

## Glossary

- **Better Cotton Initiative (BCI)**: An organization promoting sustainable cotton production practices.
- **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**: An ecosystem-based strategy focusing on long-term prevention of pests through a combination of techniques.
- **Highly Hazardous Pesticides (HHPs)**: Pesticides that present a serious risk to health and the environment.
- **Globally Harmonized System (GHS)**: A system for classifying and labeling chemicals according to their health and safety impacts.

## Benchmarking Process

The benchmarking process involved a comprehensive review of Cotton Standard indicators against the EUDR criteria. The aim was to evaluate whether the indicators align with the regulation's objectives and standards. This process included the collection and comparison of relevant data, indicator level assessment, and the establishment of alignment levels. The analysis used AI tools to classify the indicators, followed by a manual review to provide justifications and implications for each alignment level. This process ensures transparency and consistency throughout the assessment, although it remains subject to further expert validation and public consultation for finalization.
