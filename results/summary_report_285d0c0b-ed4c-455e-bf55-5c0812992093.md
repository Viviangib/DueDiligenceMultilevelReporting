The preliminary benchmarking analysis evaluates the alignment of the User Standard with the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR). It highlights specific differences at the indicator level. A round of expert review or a public consultation is recommended to verify the results generated.

### Benchmarking Criteria

The benchmarking criteria used are as follows:

| Alignment Level    | Definition                                                                                                                                                      | Implication                                                                                               |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Not applicable     | The regulation does not address the topic of this indicator of the assessed framework OR this indicator is out of scope of the regulation.                     | -                                                                                                         |
| Not aligned/Not covered | This indicator of the assessed framework is required by the regulation, but it is missing in the assessed framework. OR it contradicts the regulation. | The requirements in the assessed standard are not present. Need to be reviewed or included.               |
| Partially aligned  | This indicator of the assessed framework includes requirements similar to the regulation, but to a limited extent.                                               | Some aspects are missing and need to be reviewed.                                                         |
| Mostly aligned     | This indicator is mostly covered by the requirements in the regulation, with minor aspects differing.                                                            | Considered to be covered by the benchmarked standard, and only minor aspects are different or missing.    |
| Fully aligned      | This indicator fully matches or is equivalent to the regulation, covering the same scope and without deviation.                                                 | The requirement in the assessed standard is equivalent to this regulatory criterion. Fully covered.       |

## Recommendations

1. **Address Gaps**: Some indicators are partially aligned or not aligned. Focus on enhancing coverage, particularly where the scope of regulations extends beyond current standards.
2. **Incorporate Comprehensive Measures**: Adapt strategies to include comprehensive measures for sustainability, emphasizing broader aspects like living wages, climate change adaptation, and environmental conservation.
3. **Enhance Documentation Processes**: Strengthen systems for documentation and reporting to meet regulatory demands for transparency and detailed record-keeping.
4. **Regular Review**: Introduce regular reviews and updates of the standards to ensure ongoing alignment with regulatory changes and new industry practices.

## Glossary

- **Highly Hazardous Pesticides (HHPs)**: Pesticides associated with significant health and environmental risks. 
- **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**: A strategy that uses a combination of practices to control pests, minimizing the use of harmful pesticides.
- **Living wage**: A wage sufficient to afford a basic but decent standard of living.
- **Due diligence**: A systematic process of managing risks, ensuring compliance, and verifying adherence to legal and ethical standards.

## Benchmarking Process

The benchmarking process involved comparing the User Standard’s indicators with the EUDR’s requirements using AI-enabled analysis. Each indicator was assessed across multiple dimensions, such as scope, alignment level, and evidence used to support compliance. This analysis aimed to identify how well the User Standard aligns with regulatory expectations, providing a foundation for improvements. Recommendations were based on areas of partial or non-alignment, guiding the enhancement of voluntary sustainability standards to fulfill international regulatory criteria effectively.
