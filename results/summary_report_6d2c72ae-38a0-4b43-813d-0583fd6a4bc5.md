## Recommendations
### Potential gaps
Based on the analysis results, the following critical gaps in alignment have been identified:

- **Gender Equality**: Indicators focusing on ensuring equal participation and recognition of women are missing and should be incorporated to align with regulatory standards.
- **Labor Rights Protections**: Certain labor practices, such as anti-discrimination policies and equal pay requirements, need explicit coverage in the standards.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Broader engagement strategies emphasizing multi-stakeholder collaboration and consultation must be enhanced to meet regulatory targets.
- **Comprehensive Climate Change Adaptation**: While some environmental aspects are covered, full alignment with climate adaptation and sustainability transition plans is required.

The identified areas should be prioritized for improvement to ensure the benchmarked standard meets or exceeds regulatory criteria. Establishing clear policies on these elements and integrating them into regular monitoring and review processes is recommended.

## Appendix 1: Glossary
- **Highly Hazardous Pesticides (HHPs)**: Chemicals posing significant risks to health and the environment, requiring mindful management and phase-out strategies in agriculture.
- **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**: An ecological approach to pest management that combines various management practices and control methods.
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Safety gear and equipment used to protect individuals from health and safety risks, particularly in handling hazardous substances.
- **Due Diligence**: A risk management approach requiring companies to identify, prevent, and mitigate social and environmental risks across their operations and supply chains.
