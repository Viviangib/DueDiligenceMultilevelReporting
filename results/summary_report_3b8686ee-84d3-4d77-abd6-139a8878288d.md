## Benchmarking Results

The preliminary benchmarking analysis evaluates the incorporation of the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) intent into the Cotton Standard. The results are presented at an indicator level with labels that demonstrate the alignment between the Cotton Standard and EUDR.

### Benchmarking Criteria
- **Not applicable:** The regulation does not address or is out of scope for the indicator in the assessed framework.
  
- **Not aligned/Not covered:** This indicator is required by the regulation but is missing or contradicts the regulation in the assessed framework.
  
- **Partially aligned:** Some requirements are similar to the regulation but to a limited or substantially lower extent.
  
- **Mostly aligned:** The intent is adequately mentioned in the regulation, with minor differences.
  
- **Fully aligned:** The requirements fully match or are equivalent to the regulation, covering the same scope and extent without deviation.

### Summary:
- **Not applicable:** 2%
- **Not aligned/Not covered:** 18%
- **Partially aligned:** 40%
- **Mostly aligned:** 34%
- **Fully aligned:** 6%

These results are indicative and should undergo a round of expert review or public consultation for verification.

## Recommendations

1. **Enhance Documentation:**
   - Include more direct references to the regulation's broader context, such as comprehensive approaches beyond pesticide management to encompass economic and living wage dimensions.

2. **Expand Scope:**
   - Broaden the standard’s existing environmental and social provisions to align better with comprehensive regulatory requirements, like holistic management of climate change impacts and detailed stakeholder engagement processes.

3. **Strengthen Evidence of Compliance:**
   - Incorporate diverse and robust mechanisms to ensure compliance and alignment in practice, not just in theory.

## Glossary

- **Highly Hazardous Pesticides (HHPs):** Pesticides associated with significant health and environmental risks.
- **Integrated Pest Management (IPM):** A pest control strategy that uses a variety of complementary strategies: mechanical devices, physical devices, genetic, biological, cultural management, and chemical management.
- **Living Wages:** A wage that is high enough to maintain a normal standard of living.
- **Sustainability Practices:** Practices that contribute to the long-term ecological balance.

## Benchmarking Process

The benchmarking process involves examining the Cotton Standard against the criteria established under the EUDR. Key indicators were analyzed to ascertain alignment levels through AI-driven evaluations, identifying how comprehensively the standards encapsulate regulatory intents. This methodological assessment aids in revealing potential gaps or compliance in addressing environmental, social, and economic dimensions stipulated by the EUDR. The analysis focuses primarily on theoretical consistency across standard components rather than practical enforcement.
