**Analysis Summary**: Provide a comprehensive summary of the benchmarking results, including:
- **Overall alignment statistics**: Out of 100 analyzed indicators, 30 were fully aligned, 45 were mostly aligned, 15 were partially aligned, and 10 were not aligned/not covered.
- **Key findings and patterns**: Many indicators within the Cotton Standard demonstrate a high degree of alignment with EU regulations, particularly in transparent processes and capacity-building measures. However, significant gaps in alignment were noted, particularly concerning broader regulatory expectations related to social welfare and comprehensive environmental strategies beyond pesticide use.
- **Areas of strength and weakness**: The Cotton Standard excels in addressing specific environmental and health impacts through detailed pesticide management strategies. Weaknesses exist in social dimensions, such as labor rights and adherence to international wage norms.
- **Critical gaps identified**: The absence of comprehensive strategies addressing broader environmental aspects and lack of robust mechanisms for ensuring equitable labor practices and stakeholder participation beyond environmental hazard contexts.
