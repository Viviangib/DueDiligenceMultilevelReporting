## Benchmarking Results
The preliminary benchmarking analysis aimed to evaluate whether the overall intent of relevant criteria in the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) has been successfully incorporated into the benchmarked Cotton Standard. The analysis highlighted specific differences at the indicator level. To enhance the accuracy of these findings, a subsequent expert review or public consultation is considered necessary. The benchmark results are presented at the indicator level, employing several labels and numberings to describe the alignment between the benchmarked standard's indicators and the EUDR scope. The criteria used are:

| Alignment Level      | Definition                                                                                         | Implication                                                             |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Not applicable**   | The regulation does not address the topic of this indicator of the assessed framework OR it is out of scope. | -                                                                      |
| **Not aligned/Not covered** | This indicator is required by the regulation, but is missing in the assessed standard, or it contradicts the regulation. | The requirements are not present and need to be reviewed or included.  |
| **Partially aligned**| Requirements similar to the regulation are included, but to a limited or substantially lower extent. | Some aspects are missing and need review.                              |
| **Mostly aligned**   | The indicator is mostly covered by the regulation, with minor differences.                         | Considered covered by the standard, with minor differences.            |
| **Fully aligned**    | The indicator fully matches the regulation, covering the same scope and extent without deviation.  | Considered fully covered by the standard.                              |
